GLORB // EMPATHY MISSION

Purpose

Students practise noticing more than one clue, using careful feeling language, checking what another person needs and choosing a helpful response.

Empathy rule

NOTICE → GUESS → CHECK → RESPOND

Feeling guesses are possibilities, not facts. Students are encouraged to check rather than assume.

How to support

Let the student try first. If they need help, ask: “What clue do you notice?” or “Do we know what the person wants yet?”

Important

This is structured practice, not a test of whether a student is “empathetic”. Real people can show feelings in different ways.
